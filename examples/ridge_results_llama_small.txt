========================================================
OBSERVATIONS & ANALYSIS
========================================================

SEVERE RECALL DEFICIT IN VANILLA MODE
  Ridge-Vanilla recall=0.465 — the model finds only 46% of hate comments.
  It predicts only 6806 positives vs 10437 actual (35% under-prediction).
  This is a direct consequence of the near-zero sentiment correlation and
  status sign reversal: the Ridge learns to over-rely on the remaining good
  attributes (insult, respect), which are insufficient to recover recall.

  Persona mode partially corrects this: recall improves to 0.554, predicted
  positives increase to 8692. The partial sentiment recovery (-0.134 → -0.453
  from persona annotation) allows the Ridge to suppress fewer true positives.

PERSONA IMPROVES F1 DESPITE AUROC DROP
  Ridge-Persona F1=0.604 > Vanilla F1=0.563 (+0.041), but AUROC 0.864 <
  0.867 (-0.003). The recall improvement from persona outweighs the slight
  precision loss at threshold 0.5. However, the rank ordering of scores
  (AUROC) is marginally worse — persona adds noise at the tails of the
  distribution even as it recovers the center.

FORMULA D CATASTROPHIC FAILURE
  Formula D (Spearman-only, no Ridge, no confidence, standard): R²=-17.08.
  This is the worst formula result across all models and modes by a large
  margin. The reason: Spearman pre-scaling uses the correlations computed
  from standard annotations, which include the wrong-sign status (+0.37
  instead of ~-0.54). Pre-multiplying by the wrong-sign weight amplifies
  a bad signal. Ridge corrects this by learning to down-weight or invert it;
  Spearman-only weighting cannot.

  This confirms that Ridge's regularization is essential when attributes
  contain sign errors or near-zero signals — it selectively suppresses
  bad features while formula-based approaches cannot.

FORMULA B/C PEARSON: 0.791 (standard) vs 0.781 (persona)
  Counterintuitively, standard Ridge achieves better Pearson than persona
  despite persona producing better F1. The explanation: Pearson measures
  global linear correlation (including tails), while F1 is threshold-
  sensitive. Persona's sentiment improvement raises recall at threshold
  but doesn't improve the full rank ordering — the AUROC and Pearson
  tell the same story.

SMALL MODEL GAP
  Best small Llama Ridge AUROC=0.867 vs best big Llama=0.903. A gap of
  3.6 percentage points entirely attributable to the two broken attributes
  (sentiment, status). If those two attributes performed at big-model level,
  the small model would likely close most of this gap.
