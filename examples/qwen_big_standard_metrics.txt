COMPARISON WITH LLAMA-3.1-70B STANDARD
  Qwen2.5-72B standard slightly outperforms Llama on most attributes:
    sentiment  : -0.728 vs -0.705  (+0.02)
    respect    : -0.744 vs -0.730  (+0.01)
    violence   :  0.612 vs  0.618  (-0.01)
    genocide   :  0.526 vs  0.549  (-0.02)
  The two models are closely matched but Qwen edges ahead on
  protective attributes (sentiment, respect) while Llama leads on
  violence and genocide.

BEST DIRECT CLASSIFICATION
  hatespeech F1=0.867 is the highest direct classification score
  across all models and modes, including big models. Combined with
  MAE=0.978 (lowest among big models), Qwen2.5-72B standard shows
  the best attribute-level hate detection without Ridge.

MAE / RMSE OBSERVATIONS
  insult MAE (0.67) and genocide MAE (0.49) are the lowest across all
  big models — Qwen is notably well-calibrated on these two binary/
  near-binary scales. violence MAE (0.69) is also better than Llama.
  sentiment and respect remain high-MAE (~2.85, ~2.72) due to scale
  range, but lower than Llama's (~3.01, ~2.91).

STATUS ATTRIBUTE
  status correlation (-0.54) is weak and consistent with Llama.
  This attribute is difficult for all large models — the 0/1 binary
  scale has limited variance and "derogatory status" overlaps
  conceptually with insult and dehumanize.

STANDARD vs PERSONA (preview)
  Unlike Llama, Qwen shows almost no gain from persona mode (see
  qwen_big_persona_metrics.txt). F1 is essentially identical
  (0.8671 vs 0.8672). Qwen may already saturate the available
  signal at 72B without needing annotator context.
