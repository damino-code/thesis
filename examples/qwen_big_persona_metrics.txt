PERSONA EFFECT — NEGLIGIBLE FOR QWEN
  Persona mode produces virtually no change for Qwen2.5-72B:
    hatespeech F1 : 0.8671 → 0.8672  (+0.0001, negligible)
    sentiment     : -0.728 → -0.717   (-0.011, slight regression)
    respect       : -0.744 → -0.742   (-0.002, essentially flat)
    violence      :  0.612 →  0.608   (-0.003, essentially flat)
    genocide      :  0.526 →  0.533   (+0.007, marginal)
  Changes are negligible across the board. Qwen2.5-72B does not
  meaningfully benefit from annotator persona context — it extracts
  the same signal from text alone.

WHY PERSONA HELPS LLAMA BUT NOT QWEN
  A possible explanation: Qwen's instruction-following may parse
  the persona context differently or weight it less in its attention
  compared to Llama. At 72B both models are near-saturated on text
  signal, but Llama's architecture may integrate demographic context
  more effectively into attribute scoring.

STATUS ATTRIBUTE
  status moves from -0.54 (standard) to -0.55 (persona) — minimal
  change. Across all experiments status is the most inconsistent
  attribute, and persona context does not resolve this.

RIDGE CONSEQUENCE
  Because persona offers no attribute improvements over standard,
  the Ridge results reflect this: persona F1=0.676 < vanilla F1=0.692.
  For Qwen, vanilla is the better mode for the full pipeline, even
  though AUROC is marginally higher in persona (0.895 vs 0.893).
