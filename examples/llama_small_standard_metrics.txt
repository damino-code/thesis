OBSERVATIONS & ANALYSIS
========================================================

CRITICAL FAILURE: SENTIMENT
  sentiment Spearman=-0.016 — near zero.
  The 8B model in vanilla mode essentially produces random scores
  for the sentiment attribute. The large model (70B) achieves -0.770
  on the same attribute. This is the most dramatic gap between
  model sizes in this study.

  A likely cause: the sentiment prompt uses a 0-4 scale where the
  small model outputs tokens inconsistently — possibly defaulting to
  a single value or producing scale-reversed responses. The Spearman
  of -0.016 suggests no monotonic relationship at all.

STATUS SIGN REVERSAL
  status Spearman=+0.39 — positive instead of negative.
  Big models correctly treat high status scores as protective (negative
  correlation with hate_speech_score). The small model inverts this,
  suggesting it misunderstands the status prompt direction. This
  contaminates Ridge features with a wrong-sign signal.

STRONG ATTRIBUTES DESPITE SIZE
  Despite the sentiment and status failures, insult (0.68), respect
  (-0.69), humiliate (0.65) and attack_defend (0.56) remain reasonably
  strong. The model retains genuine signal on more concrete/explicit
  attributes even at 8B.

  hatespeech F1=0.861 — surprisingly close to big model (0.820-0.867).
  The direct yes/no hate judgment is more robust than continuous scoring.

MAE PARADOX
  sentiment MAE=1.16 and status MAE=0.63 are much lower than the big
  model's equivalent (~3.0 and ~2.0). This reflects scale collapse:
  the small model outputs a narrow range of values, leading to low
  absolute error but also near-zero correlation. Low MAE here does
  NOT mean better annotation — it means the model is not varying
  its responses appropriately across the dataset.

RIDGE CONSEQUENCE
  The near-zero sentiment and wrong-sign status degrade Ridge quality.
  OOF Pearson drops to 0.791 vs 0.840 for the big Llama model.
  Ridge F1=0.563 (vanilla) is markedly lower than the big model.
