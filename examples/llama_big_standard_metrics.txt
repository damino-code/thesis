OBSERVATIONS & ANALYSIS
========================================================

STRONGEST ATTRIBUTES
  sentiment (-0.70) and respect (-0.73) are the two best predictors of
  hate_speech_score. Their negative sign is expected: high sentiment
  (positive tone) and high respect both correlate with low hate. Together
  they act as the model's strongest protective-attribute signal.

  insult (0.70) and humiliate (0.66) are the strongest hate-positive
  attributes, closely followed by attack_defend (0.64) and violence (0.62).
  These capture the most direct markers of hateful content.

WEAKEST / PROBLEMATIC ATTRIBUTE
  hatespeech (-0.44) — the model's own hate-speech judgment has the
  weakest and also negative correlation. The prompt scale is 0=no hate,
  1=unclear, 2=hate — so higher values should correlate positively with
  hate_speech_score. The observed negative sign indicates systematic
  under-scoring: the model tends to output low values (0 or 1) even for
  text with high IRT hate scores, suggesting poor sensitivity at the upper
  end of the hate continuum. Despite this, direct F1=0.82 on the binary
  hatespeech attribute shows the model classifies hate reasonably well when
  evaluated at the correct threshold — the continuous score range is just
  poorly calibrated.

  status (Spearman=-0.57) is the weakest among the
  hate-positive attributes. The 0/1 binary scale makes it harder to
  calibrate, and the concept of "derogatory status" overlaps with
  other dimensions.

MAE / RMSE OBSERVATIONS
  sentiment and respect have very high MAE (~3.0) and RMSE (~3.2)
  because they use a 0-4 scale while human annotations span a wide
  range — the model tends to use extreme values. In contrast, insult,
  violence, and genocide have MAE < 0.8, indicating better calibration
  on their narrower scales.

STANDARD vs PERSONA (preview)
  In vanilla mode this model already achieves strong correlations.
  Persona mode (see llama_big_persona_metrics.txt) brings marginal
  improvement: +0.02 on sentiment Spearman, +0.02 on insult, +0.04
  on attack_defend. The gains suggest annotator context helps but
  the baseline is already well-calibrated for a 70B model.
