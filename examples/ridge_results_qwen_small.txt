========================================================
OBSERVATIONS & ANALYSIS
========================================================

VANILLA STRICTLY BETTER THAN PERSONA
  For Qwen 7B, vanilla outperforms persona on every metric:
    F1      : 0.561 vs 0.546  (-0.015)
    AUROC   : 0.861 vs 0.858  (-0.003)
    Pearson : 0.778 vs 0.761  (-0.017)
  This directly follows the per-attribute degradation in persona mode
  (see qwen_small_persona_metrics.txt): the status sign reversal and
  sentiment regression under persona translate to a weaker Ridge input.
  The recommended pipeline for Qwen 7B is vanilla in all experiments.

COMPARISON WITH SMALL LLAMA
  Qwen 7B standard vs Llama 8B standard: AUROC 0.861 vs 0.867.
  Small Llama vanilla has slightly higher AUROC despite its catastrophic
  sentiment failure. This happens because Llama's good attributes (insult,
  violence, genocide) are stronger (0.71, 0.69, 0.70) than Qwen 7B's
  (0.66, 0.57, 0.49), partially compensating in AUROC. However, Qwen 7B
  persona beats Llama 8B persona on AUROC (0.858 vs 0.864 — both degrade
  from persona, Llama less so).

  Best small model overall: Llama-3.1-8B persona (AUROC=0.864, F1=0.604).

FORMULA D: STILL VERY NEGATIVE
  Standard formula D: R²=-9.14. Persona formula D: R²=-8.31.
  The Spearman-only combination without Ridge remains catastrophic. Even
  Qwen 7B's weaker but correct-sign attributes produce a bad unweighted
  sum — Ridge regularization is indispensable regardless of model quality.

  The smaller magnitude of the D failure compared to small Llama (-9.1 vs
  -17.1) reflects that Qwen 7B avoids the extreme sign errors of Llama 8B
  standard — the damage is real but less severe.

SIZE VS ARCHITECTURE TAKEAWAY
  Comparing small models: Qwen 7B has better direct classification (F1=0.889)
  but similar Ridge AUROC (0.861 vs 0.867). The discrepancy reveals that
  the Ridge pipeline and direct hatespeech attribute diverge: direct
  classification captures Qwen's strong binary judgment, while Ridge
  aggregates all 10 attributes where Llama's stronger insult/violence
  attributes offer compensating signal.

SCALE GAP
  Qwen 7B AUROC=0.861 vs Qwen 72B AUROC=0.893 — a 3.2 point gap from
  10x parameter scaling. The gap is smaller than Llama's 3.6 point gap
  (8B vs 70B), suggesting Qwen extracts relatively more signal per parameter
  at the smaller scale, but neither small model approaches big-model quality.
