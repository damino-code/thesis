OBSERVATIONS & ANALYSIS
========================================================

PERSONA PARTIALLY RECOVERS SENTIMENT
  sentiment Spearman improves from -0.016 (standard) to -0.433 (persona) —
  a large absolute gain (+0.417), but still far from the big model (-0.724).
  Persona context (annotator demographics) provides the small model with
  enough framing to avoid pure random scoring, but the 8B capacity remains
  a hard ceiling on how well it handles the 0-4 sentiment scale.

  hatespeech F1 drops from 0.861 → 0.846. Despite sentiment improvement,
  the direct hate judgment is slightly degraded — persona context may
  introduce noise that disrupts the binary calibration.

STATUS: WRONG SIGN TO NEAR-ZERO
  status moves from +0.37 (standard, wrong sign) to +0.024 (persona, near-
  zero). The persona prompt partially suppresses the directional error but
  does not correct it. The model remains unable to reliably interpret
  "derogatory status" direction at 8B — it neither inverts correctly nor
  produces a meaningful signal.

PERSONA HURTS MOST OTHER ATTRIBUTES
  Contrary to the big Llama model where persona brings uniform small gains,
  the 8B model regresses on most attributes in persona mode:
    insult      : 0.675 → 0.566  (-0.109)
    humiliate   : 0.648 → 0.518  (-0.130)
    dehumanize  : 0.554 → 0.512  (-0.042)
    violence    : 0.550 → 0.485  (-0.065)
    genocide    : 0.430 → 0.411  (-0.019)
  The small model cannot simultaneously process annotator demographics and
  attribute-specific text signals — the persona context crowds out the
  attribute reasoning capacity.

  respect Spearman worsens (-0.703 → -0.671 — regression).
  No attribute shows meaningful improvement in persona except sentiment.

RIDGE CONSEQUENCE: F1 IMPROVES, AUROC DROPS
  Despite per-attribute regressions, Ridge F1 improves: 0.563 → 0.604 in
  persona mode. AUROC drops slightly: 0.867 → 0.864.

  The F1 gain comes from better sentiment — the Ridge's most-weighted
  negative predictor. Even an imperfect -0.453 correlation on sentiment
  is far better than near-zero, allowing the Ridge to correctly suppress
  some true-negative predictions. AUROC drop reflects that the overall
  rank ordering of scores is slightly worse due to the other attribute
  regressions.

  Key takeaway: for small Llama, persona mode is net positive for F1-based
  classification but net neutral/slightly negative for ranking quality.
