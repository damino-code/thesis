  demographic context appears to degrade status signal in small models
  regardless of architecture.

PATTERN ACROSS SMALL MODELS
  Persona improves small Llama (especially sentiment: -0.016 → -0.433).
  Persona hurts small Qwen (sentiment: -0.596 → -0.567).
  The contrast suggests the two architectures respond differently to
  persona context at 7-8B scale: Llama benefits from the added framing
  on its weakest attribute, while Qwen already handles sentiment well
  in standard mode and the persona context only adds noise.

RIDGE CONSEQUENCE
  Ridge persona underperforms vanilla for Qwen 7B: F1 0.561 → 0.546,
  AUROC 0.861 → 0.858. This parallels the big Qwen result — persona is
  the wrong mode for Qwen regardless of scale. For Qwen, vanilla is
  strictly preferred for the Ridge pipeline.
